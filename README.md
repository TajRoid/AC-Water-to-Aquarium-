FlowCollect: Gather AC condensate in a clean, covered container.Pre-Filter: Pass water through filter cotton and activated carbon to remove dust and trace heavy metals.Aerator Cycle: Aerate for 24 hours to stabilize gas levels and boost dissolved oxygen.Automated Dosing (SPARK System):Select Fish or Plant mode on OLED display.System continuously checks live pH and TDS.Automatically triggers 360° servos to dose Mineral and pH Buffer until parameters hit the target range.Validation: Introduce indicator fish (e.g., Betta/Molly) for 3–7 days.⚠️ Essential RulesNever use 100% raw AC water: Lacks essential minerals ($\text{Ca}^{2+}$, $\text{Mg}^{2+}$) and will cause fatal osmotic shock to fish.Always aerate first: Raw condensate is stagnant and depleted of dissolved oxygen.Keep pipes clean: Regularly clean the AC drain hose to prevent mold/bacterial contamination.
