Automatic Fish Feeder by coberdas on Thingiverse: https://www.thingiverse.com/thing:301532

Summary:
This is still a work in progress but I have printed and assembled.  I created a automatic fish feeder that will use a arduino board that will rotate the auger every 12 hours to feed my fish.  I will also have a manual override switch to feed the fish at any time.  My old fish feeder was made out of cardboard so I thought it was time to 3D print one.Update:4/20/2014Added Arduino program for fish feeder.
